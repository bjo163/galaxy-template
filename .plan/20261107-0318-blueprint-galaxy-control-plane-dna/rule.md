# Rules

1. Design for Galaxy as a builder/control plane for workspaces, projects, models, records, and add-ons.
2. Keep the actual product workflow more visible than decorative branding.
3. Do not use generic space imagery, star fields, purple gradients, or glow as substitutes for product identity.
4. Preserve a working light/dark theme and accessible contrast, keyboard focus, and responsive layouts.
5. Do not create or commission visual assets until the owner approves the direction.
6. Keep unrelated Vuexy demo pages out of the initial redesign scope.
7. Write design artifacts in English and use repository-relative file paths.
